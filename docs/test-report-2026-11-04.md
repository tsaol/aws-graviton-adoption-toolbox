| Case | Mode | Runs | Fact score | Wrong claims / run | Key numbers right | Time (s) | Turns | Cost (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ice-after-release | **with** | 2 | 1.0 | 0 | 1.0 | 83.8 | 8.5 | 0.297 |

Fact score: covered = 1, partly = 0.5, missing = 0, averaged over the rubric facts. Key numbers right: share of facts whose numbers all appear in the answer.

### What the numbers say

- **With the toolbox, every rubric fact was covered in all 4 runs (score 1.0), with no wrong claims.** Without it, the score was 0.90-0.92, also with no wrong claims.
- **Where "without" lost points:**
  - ice-two-causes #1 called the fallback failures an AZ pool shortage and explained the free capacity in the snapshot away as an AZ-name mapping issue. It never escalated to the account team, which is the key judgment in this case.
  - ice-two-causes #2 said the Graviton rollout indirectly made the batch job's quota problem worse.
  - ice-after-release #1 and #2 did not say outright that this was not a quota problem.
  - "Key numbers right" was 0.8 in ice-two-causes without the toolbox, because the totals were not stated.
- **Speed and cost:** with the toolbox, runs were about 10-15% faster (84 vs 98 s; 94 vs 103 s) and about 10% cheaper, even with more turns (it runs scripts instead of writing them).

### Honest reading

- **The gap is small.** A strong model (Opus 5.5) already gets about 90% of these two cases right on its own. The toolbox fixes the last judgment calls (escalate when the snapshot shows free capacity; say plainly what is and isn't a quota problem) and makes the numbers come from checked scripts.
- **The cases are probably too easy.** The task text mentions the merged trails and the snapshot, which hints at both traps. Real requests won't do that.
- **Small sample:** 2 runs per case and mode, 2 cases. This is not statistically meaningful.
- **Judge bias:** the judge is the same model family as the agent. The rubric and the cases were written by the toolbox author.
- **Synthetic data:** real CloudTrail exports are messier (CreateFleet errors inside responseElements, Karpenter, Spot).

### Next tests

1. Harder cases: task text without hints; CreateFleet / Karpenter events; more noise; a case where pausing *is* right.
2. Weaker or different agents (Kiro, Codex, a smaller model), where the toolbox should matter more.
3. 5+ runs per mode, and a second judge model from a different vendor.
4. Track the human baseline: how long the same diagnosis took by hand.
